CHANGES MADE (this is an adapted extract, not the original dataset):
  · restricted to rank "species" in five groups — Insecta, Mammalia, Amphibia
    (class) and Plantae, Fungi (kingdom);
  · restricted to a northern-European slice: species iNaturalist holds a
    research-grade observation of between 54-72 N and 4-32 E, plus every species it
    holds a Norwegian, Swedish, Danish, Finnish, Estonian or Lithuanian name for;
  · one name per species per language (iNaturalist's first/preferred row), the rest
    discarded;
  · re-keyed by lower-cased scientific name and re-encoded as JSON per language;
  · English omitted (the app already shows the English name).
